Need to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about Essex Roleplay.

How do I join Essex Roleplay?

Join our Discord using the invite link on this website. That's where we'll share updates, community information and confirmed access details.

Is the server open yet?

The project is in development. We'll announce public access and any recruitment openings through the official Discord.

Can I apply for a department?

Keep an eye on Discord announcements and application channels for confirmed vacancies and application instructions.

Where can I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use the relevant development or support channels in our Discord so the team can review your feedback.

Is this an official Essex public-service website?

No. Essex Roleplay is an independent gaming community and is not affiliated with any real-world police, fire, ambulance or government organisation.

Where will launch news be posted?

All confirmed launch updates will be shared in the official Essex Roleplay Discord.
